Read an AQL table in five minutes

Find your lot size, read the sample size, then the accept and reject numbers. For a 1,000-piece lot at AQL 2.5, level II, inspect 80 pieces: accept up to 5 defective, reject at 6. The AQL tool in the Garment kit does the lookup; confirm the level with your buyer.

Price your freelance hour

Add your monthly income goal and costs, divide by the billable hours in a month (hours per week × 4.3), then add a 15% buffer. A $1,500 goal plus $200 costs at 20 billable hours a week comes to about $22.73 an hour.

Work backwards from every scholarship deadline

Have documents ready 4 weeks before the deadline and request reference letters 6 weeks before. Add each deadline in the Student kit and Taskifyra alerts you before it arrives.
